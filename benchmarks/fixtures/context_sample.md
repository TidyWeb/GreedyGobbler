# Field note: anaerobic digestion trials

The opening calibration suggests that the pilot vessel stabilised after the first six hours of operation. The operators recorded a pale foam layer that cleared without intervention.

At the first checkpoint, the system retained a steady temperature and the inlet flow remained unchanged. This ordinary observation is included as surrounding context.

## Trial North

The North trial recorded a methane yield of 18.4 litres per kilogram of volatile solids. The sample was taken after 24 hours at 37 degrees Celsius.

The yield does not establish that every batch will reach the same value. The feedstock mix and the sampling interval remained specific to this trial.

## Trial South

The South trial also recorded a yield of 18.4 litres per kilogram of volatile solids, but at 35 degrees Celsius. This is a separate repeated result, not a duplicate measurement.

The South sample excludes the first six hours of start-up. Do not compare it directly with the North trial's full 24-hour window.

The dosing note says that the inhibitor was absent from the control vessel. The treated vessel contained 2.5 milligrams per litre.

## Handling exception

Samples were refrigerated before analysis, except the late-shift sample, which remained at room temperature for 40 minutes.

The late-shift result is not evidence of a general temperature effect. The handling exception may explain its lower reading, but the note does not establish causation.

The instrument's stated tolerance was plus or minus 0.2 litres per kilogram. The team retained the original units in the notebook.
